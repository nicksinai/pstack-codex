- C1: [official Codex skills](https://developers.openai.com/codex/skills) establishes skill files, explicit invocation and `agents/openai.yaml` with `allow_implicit_invocation:false`. Direct sibling reads avoid dependence on catalog capacity or a universal Skill tool. Frontmatter, namespacing and explicit policy are established by inspection. Recipient file access and probabilistic instruction adherence remain conditional.
- C2: [official Codex subagents](https://developers.openai.com/codex/subagents) supports delegated work, separate model/effort selection and explicit scoped briefs. The shared delegation/models contracts bind actual exposed schemas, local worktrees, resource ownership, independent GPT reviewers, fresh/resume semantics and limited nesting. Tools, permissions, available models and result/cancellation operations must be checked at runtime. They are not established by mentioning them.
- C3: [continuation](../references/continuation.md) owns invocation state, active waits, private checkpoint and recovery. [Official scheduling](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/automations) requires an available desktop/computer/project and does not establish progress through sleep/app closure. Sticky persistence and recovery after lost context are conditional on a valid scoped user entry record. No native Cursor mode/reminder or durable schedule from an ordinary run is claimed.
- C4: [PR delivery](../references/pr-delivery.md) owns cleanup positions, forge/PR operations, independent shipping evidence, authority and watcher prerequisites. [Official plugin guidance](https://developers.openai.com/plugins/build/plugins) establishes packaging, not recipient forge credentials or installation. Ready PR delivery remains conditional on actual forge access and user scope. Source/config review never substitutes for required workflow reproduction/live verification.
- C5: Real-artifact verification, local scripts and TypeScript rules are preserved instruction requirements. Recipient runtime, dependencies, control adapters and real target access are conditional. Missing control instructions block the required lane; INCONCLUSIVE and wrong-surface remain non-passing.
- C6: [history](../references/history.md), [session pickup](../references/session-pickup.md), [authoring](../references/skill-authoring.md) and [reflect](../skills/pstack-reflect/SKILL.md) own scoped source/history, continuation and durable-learning approvals. Unavailable records or mutation authority are reported rather than assumed.
